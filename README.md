# Solar Powered Grass Cutter Machine ☀️🌿

**A compact, eco-friendly grass cutting machine powered entirely by solar energy.**

No grid connection. No fuel. No exhaust emissions during operation.

---

## Introduction

A solar powered grass cutter is a machine used to cut grass and small weeds using a rotating cutting blade. This is a compact and eco-friendly grass cutting machine where the electrical energy required for cutting is obtained from a solar panel and stored in a rechargeable battery.

The machine consists of a solar panel, rechargeable battery, DC motor, cutting blade, motor shaft, blade guard, wheels, frame, handle, switch and charging/control circuit. The DC motor rotates the cutting blade at high speed, cutting grass efficiently. Solar energy is converted into electrical energy to operate the motor — reducing dependence on conventional electricity.

## Working Principle

```
1. Solar panel receives sunlight
        ↓
2. Converts sunlight to electrical energy
        ↓
3. Solar charge controller safely charges the battery
        ↓
4. Battery stores energy
        ↓
5. ON/OFF switch turned ON → battery supplies DC motor
        ↓
6. Motor rotates cutting blade at high speed via motor shaft
        ↓
7. Operator moves machine using handle and wheels
        ↓
8. Rotating blade cuts the grass
        ↓
9. Blade guard provides protection from accidental contact
```

Solar energy operates the grass cutter with less manual effort and without direct connection to conventional electrical power during operation.

## Conclusion

The solar powered grass cutter is a simple, compact and eco-friendly machine designed for efficient grass cutting. The project reduces manual effort, produces no direct exhaust emissions during operation and can be effectively used for gardens, lawns, agricultural areas and other small-scale grass cutting applications.
